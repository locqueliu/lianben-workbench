人物：林砚 苏晚

林砚（对白，声线压低）：这雾不对。
LIN YAN (DIALOGUE, voice low): Stay close to me. That name is on the list.

LIN YAN (DIALOGUE, tail held back): We have three hours. That name is on the list.

LIN YAN (DIALOGUE, breath short): Check the north gate. That name is on the list.
